Rhythm becomes pitch. Repeat a hit twice per second and you hear a beat. Repeat it twenty times per second and the hits fuse into a low tone; at two hundred times per second it is a clear note. Pulse and pitch are one periodicity seen at two scales. Flutter (row 8) is the element that lives on that border, between Order and Tonal.

Instrument fingerprints

There are thousands of instruments, but they come from six ways of exciting a vibrating body. Each family shares a backbone of elements; each instrument adds a few of its own. Recognising an instrument then means measuring which elements are present and how strongly, the way a spectrum line identifies a chemical element. Separation is the next step: break the mix into elements, then rebuild each instrument from its fingerprint. The kick portrait in our stem experiments was the first one rebuilt this way.
