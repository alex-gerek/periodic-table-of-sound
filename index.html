Periodic Table of Sound

The elements of music, ordered by a number you can measure. Pitch, pulse, vibrato, bar and phrase are placed by their own time, grouped by the question they ask of the signal, and checked on real recordings. The empty cells are predictions, and a collider builds them from scratch.

Five rules

OrderTime octave Z: one step doubles τ. Music spans about 23 steps.
GroupSix questions: Level, Periodicity, Irregularity, Change, Makeup, Space.
PeriodBorders of hearing, drawn as zones about ±0.3 Z wide.
StabilityA defect is an unstable form of a healthy element.
Metric intervalΔZ to the pulse: log₂ 4 for a 4/4 bar, log₂ 3 for a waltz.
