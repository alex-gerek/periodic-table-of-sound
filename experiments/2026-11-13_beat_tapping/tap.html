Tap the Beat

13 short excerpts, two passes each, about 15 minutes. Tap a steady, even pulse, like a metronome or your foot while walking: the same gap every time, even through breaks. Do not follow the drum pattern. There is no right speed, and the page does not show any tempo.

How it works
  1. Pick a track, press Start pass, and listen for a few seconds.
  2. Tap the big button, or press Space, on every beat until the excerpt ends (30 s).
  3. Then the second pass of the same track. If the two passes agree, the track is done.
  4. If you cannot feel a steady beat, press No steady beat.
